Every run of text on this page asks for Apple’s system face before anything else. On a Mac that is SF Pro. In the Linux container it is whatever the fallback stack reaches first, and the two do not have the same metrics — so a line that just fits on one platform may not on the other.

Where a substitution shows first

Narrow glyphs are where the difference bites: i, l, t, f and the figures. A heading set tight enough to fill its measure will re-wrap when the face changes, and a button label sized to its text will change width with it.

A paragraph long enough to wrap several times

Pre-assembled boiler boards and complete hydronic mechanical packages are specified by the room they serve, not by the panel that heats it. A high-performance radiant floor system requires more than quality panels and tubing: it needs the manifold, the mixing assembly and the controls to agree with one another before anyone pours a slab over the top of them.
